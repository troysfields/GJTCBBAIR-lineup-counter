What the numbers mean
Match Win % = chance of winning 2 out of 3 lines (singles + D1 + D2). This is what matters.
Line Win % = chance of winning that specific court. Green = 60%+, Yellow = 40-60%, Red = under 40%.
"Concede line" = the algorithm intentionally accepts losing that court to dominate the other two. This is often the smartest play.
Alternative Lineups = tap to expand. If the top 2 lineups are close (within 2-3%), go with your gut.

How the strength ratings work
Each player has a strength value (0 to 1) calculated from three data sources:
• Game win % (40% weight) — most granular indicator. A player who loses 7-5, 7-6 is very different from 6-0, 6-1.
• Dynamic rating (30% weight) — TennisRecord.com's ELO-style estimate based on who you beat.
• Match win % (30% weight) — straight W-L record.

Level adjustment: Ashley Rossman's 2025 record (4-11) was entirely at 3.0 level against much stronger opponents (DR 2.48-2.98). Her 2.5-level record is 10-6 (62.5%) — that's what we use.

Blending: 2026 results get 15% weight (small sample), career stats at the correct level get primary weight. DR normalization maps the 2.0-2.5 range for this league.

Head-to-head: Where available, scouting notes include specific past matchups between our players and theirs.

Win probability model: Equal strength = 50/50. A 0.10 strength edge ≈ 62% win chance. A 0.20 edge ≈ 73%. Doubles averages both players with a small chemistry bonus when two top-5 players pair.

Data source: TennisRecord.com profiles, match histories, and team pages. All stats verified with exact DR values to 4 decimal places.
